## Draft page copy

Sometimes you do not need a whole website. You need one good page.

A single, focused page can give a new business a credible home online, put a launch in front of the right people, explain one offer or act as a sensible first step before a bigger site. I design and build one-page websites for businesses across North Wales, with every word and element working towards one clear action.

### One page, one job

The first question is simple: what must this page help someone do? That might be enquire, book, buy, register interest or understand a new offer. Once that job is clear, I cut anything that gets in the way and build the page around the answer.

You get copy written for the page, a design that reflects the thing you are promoting and a build that is quick to use on a phone. The page can live on your own domain, and it can be the first page of a larger website later if that becomes the right move.

### A good fit for

- a product or service launch;
- an event or time-limited offer;
- a new business that needs a credible first page;
- a campaign that needs somewhere better to send people than a social profile;
- an idea you want to test before committing to a full site.

### What is included

- one focused page with a clear structure;
- copy written to do the page’s job;
- a mobile-friendly, fast-loading build;
- a form or button that sends people to the right next step;
- help pointing your own domain at the page;
- a route to grow into a fuller site later if needed.

### Not sure whether one page is enough?

That is exactly the sort of thing to discuss before starting. I will tell you if a single page is the sensible answer, or if your project needs a wider structure.

### Just need one good page?

Tell me what it is for, who it is aimed at and what you want people to do next. You do not need to arrive with finished copy.
