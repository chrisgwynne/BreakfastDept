## Draft page copy

If your website makes people work too hard to understand what you do, it is not doing its job. I design and build clear, fast websites for small and independent businesses across North Wales — with the words, structure and next step settled before the decoration.

Breakfast is a small, independent studio. You deal directly with Chris Gwynne, the person designing and building the site, from the first questions to launch. The work is planned around what you sell, who buys it and the one useful action a visitor needs to take.

### A website that says what you do

A good site should make the basics easy:

- understand what you offer without digging;
- find the right next step on a phone;
- trust that the site belongs to a real business;
- contact, book or buy without unnecessary friction.

I start with questions, write the copy in plain English, design around it and build the site to work properly across devices. If a smaller piece of work would do the job, I will say so rather than sell you more than you need.

### What the work can include

- strategy and page structure;
- copy written around your business and customers;
- custom design and build;
- a simple way to make everyday edits;
- privacy-respecting analytics;
- launch support and practical care afterwards.

### Who this suits

This is for small and independent businesses in North Wales that want a professional site that works, not a large agency process or a template filled with generic language. It can be a first proper website, a replacement for one that no longer fits, or a focused improvement to the site you already have.

### How it works

1. **Questions first** — we settle what you sell, who buys it and what the site needs to do.
2. **A clear plan** — each page earns its place and has a job.
3. **Written, then built** — the words lead the design; the result is tested on a phone as well as a larger screen.
4. **Launched, then looked after** — launch is not the end of the conversation.

### Thinking about a website?

Tell me a few lines about your business and what is getting in the way. You do not need a finished brief. I will give you an honest view on whether Breakfast is the right fit.
