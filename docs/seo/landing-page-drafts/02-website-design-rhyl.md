## Draft page copy

Your website is often the first place someone checks before they decide whether to get in touch. For a Rhyl business, that means making the important things easy to find: what you do, who it is for and how someone can take the next step.

I design and build clear, fast websites for small businesses across North Wales, including Rhyl. Breakfast is deliberately small, so you work directly with Chris Gwynne — the person writing, designing and building the site.

### A local page with a useful job

A good website does not need to shout. It needs to answer the questions your customers actually have. We can shape the site around your real offer, whether you need a first proper website, a replacement for one that is hard to use, or a single page for one clear service or offer.

The process starts with questions about your business and the people you want to reach. Then I plan the pages, write the words, design around them and build a site that is straightforward to use on a phone. Nothing in the process depends on pretending to be a business based in Rhyl or inventing local proof: the honest proposition is North Wales web design, delivered for businesses in Rhyl and the wider area.

### What you can expect

- a clear page structure built around one main customer action;
- copy that sounds like your business rather than brochure language;
- a site designed to work on a phone;
- an editable website for everyday changes;
- a written scope and quote before work starts;
- practical support after launch.

### Is this the right starting point?

Choose a full website if you need a considered site for your business. Choose a single landing page if you have one focused thing to promote. If your existing site is slow, broken on mobile or difficult to control, start with a website rescue conversation instead.
