## Draft page copy

Selling online should not feel like taking on a second full-time job. I design Shopify shops for makers, roasters, shops and independent businesses across North Wales — a branded storefront for your products, with the familiar Shopify admin behind it.

The point is not to make a shop that looks clever in a screenshot. It is to make the path from product to checkout clear on a phone, and to give you an online shop you can keep up to date without a maintenance headache.

### Design around what you sell

Before anything is designed, we talk about your products, how they are grouped, how you restock and how people decide to buy. Then I shape the shop around that reality: useful product pages, clear navigation, reassuring checkout steps and a visual identity that belongs to your business.

Shopify handles the boring but important parts — payments, security, tax, postage and stock tools. I handle the structure, design and setup, and help you get your first products in place. Shopify’s own monthly and payment fees are separate from the project and are explained before work starts.

### What the project can include

- a Shopify shop designed around your brand rather than a generic theme;
- product collections and pages that are straightforward to manage;
- a shopping journey designed for mobile as well as larger screens;
- support setting up products, postage and stock workflows;
- practical testing before anyone relies on the checkout;
- guidance so you can make everyday changes yourself.

### Who it suits

This is for independent makers, roasters, shops and product businesses that want to sell on their own terms. If you only need one focused page, a full shop may be more than you need; if an existing shop is slow or awkward, a rescue conversation may be the better first step.

### Ready to talk shop?

Tell me what you sell and roughly how much of it. I will come back with an honest view of what the shop would involve.
