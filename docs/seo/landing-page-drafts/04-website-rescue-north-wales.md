## Draft page copy

Not every difficult website needs replacing. Sometimes it needs someone to find out what is actually wrong.

I help businesses across North Wales with sites that are slow, awkward on a phone, out of date, hard to edit or no longer under their control. Breakfast starts with a proper diagnosis, then gives you a plain-English recommendation: fix the important problems, or start fresh if that is genuinely the more sensible route.

### Start with the problem, not a sales pitch

You might be dealing with:

- a site that takes too long to load;
- a layout that falls apart on a phone;
- wording or pages you cannot change yourself;
- a missing login or a vanished developer;
- uncertainty about whether the site is helping or getting in the way.

The first step is to understand the situation. I look at how a visitor and a search engine experience the site, separate symptoms from causes and rank the fixes by what is worth doing. If the honest answer is “not much”, you will be told that too.

### What you get

- an honest diagnosis of the site’s real problems;
- a clear, prioritised list of possible fixes;
- practical work on speed, mobile, content or access where appropriate;
- a plain-English repair-versus-rebuild recommendation;
- a separate scope and quote for any work that follows the diagnosis.

### No needless rebuild

A rescue is not a disguised sales route into a new website. A few targeted fixes may be enough. If a rebuild would be more sensible, I will explain why and leave you with a clear choice.

### Got a site that is letting you down?

Send me the address and a line about what is going wrong. I will tell you honestly whether it looks worth rescuing and what the next step could be.
