Process:

-
  title: I ask annoying questions
  text: What you sell, who buys it, and why they choose you. The least glamorous part of the job and easily the most important.
-
  title: I decide what goes where
  text: I plan each page around the one thing a visitor needs to do. Anything that doesn't help that gets cut.
-
  title: I write it, then build it
  text: Words first, design around them. Fast, clear, and working properly on a phone.
-
  title: I don't disappear
  text: I launch, watch what people actually do, and fix what's confusing.

----

Pricing_guidance: There's no fixed price, because there's no fixed website. A single clear site for a small business is a very different job from a shop with hundreds of products. I scope every project after an honest conversation about what you need, give you a written quote before anything starts, and never surprise you with a bill. If a cheaper, smaller approach would serve you better, I'll say so.

----

Timescale: Usually four to eight weeks from first conversation to launch, depending on how much writing and photography is involved and how quickly I can get feedback from you.
