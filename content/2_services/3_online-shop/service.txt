Process:

-
  title: Understand what you sell
  text: How your products are grouped, how you restock, and how people decide to buy.
-
  title: Design the shopping, not just the shop
  text: I design the whole path from landing to checkout, because that's where sales are won or lost.
-
  title: Build, load your products, launch
  text: I set it up, help you get your first products in, and make sure checkout works before anyone relies on it.

Pricing_guidance: Scoped per project, because a ten-product shop and a hundred-product shop are different animals. Shopify charges its own monthly fee and payment fees on top, which I'll always be upfront about so there are no surprises in your first month.

----

Timescale: Usually five to ten weeks, depending on how many products you have and how ready the photography is.
