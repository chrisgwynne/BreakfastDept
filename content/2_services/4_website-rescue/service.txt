Suitable_for: Anyone with an existing site that's underperforming, out of date, hard to edit, or that they've simply lost control of. People who suspect their site is fine but aren't sure why it isn't working.

----

Problems:

-
  text: Your site is slow, and you're losing people before it even loads.
-
  text: It looks broken on phones, which is where most of your visitors are.
-
  text: You can't edit it yourself, or the person who could has disappeared.

----

Deliverables:

-
  text: An honest diagnosis of what's actually holding the site back.
-
  text: A clear list of fixes, ranked by what'll make the most difference.
-
  text: The fixes themselves — speed, mobile, content, or access.
-
  text: A plain-English recommendation on whether to repair or rebuild.

----

Process:

-
  title: Diagnose properly
  text: I audit the site the way a visitor and a search engine actually experience it, and find the real problems.
-
  title: Tell you the truth
  text: A short, jargon-free report on what's wrong and what's worth fixing — including if the answer is "not much".
-
  title: Fix what matters
  text: I do the work that'll make a real difference, and skip the busywork that won't.

----

Pricing_guidance: I usually start with a fixed-price audit so you know what you're getting into for a small, known amount. Any fixes after that are scoped and quoted separately, so you're never signing a blank cheque. If the honest answer is that a rebuild would cost less than endless patching, I'll say so.

----

Timescale: The audit is quick — usually within a week or two. How long fixes take depends entirely on what I find.

----

Faqs:

-
  question: What if you find it needs a full rebuild?
  answer: I'll tell you plainly, and why. You're free to walk away with the audit and no obligation to have me do the rebuild.
-
  question: I've lost access to my own site — can you help?
  answer: Often, yes. Regaining control of your domain and hosting is one of the most common rescues I do.
