Title: The Quirky Gift Co.

----

Project_status: internal

----

Client: In-house project

----

Summary: My own Shopify store for personalised, quirky gifts — built as a revenue stream I own outright, running alongside Etsy.

----

Challenge: I was already selling personalised gifts on Etsy, and it works — but a marketplace only ever rents you the audience. It takes a cut of every order, owns the customer relationship, sets the rules, and sits my products next to everyone else's.

I wanted a second channel I actually own: my own storefront, my own brand, my own customer list and my own margins — without giving up the made-to-order, personalised range that sells.

----

Approach: I built the whole thing end to end:

- A full Shopify storefront — brand, layout, product range and the personalisation flow — stood up to run solo alongside everything else I do.
- A wide, quirky range organised into shop-by-category and shop-by-occasion, so browsing has a job rather than being a wall of products: door toppers, metal signs, wall prints, keyrings, 3D-printed gifts and license plates.
- Made-to-order personalisation on the products that need it, so a name or a place turns a stock design into a one-off.
- Product and category copy written in the brand's own voice — playful and a little cheeky — instead of flat marketplace listings.

----

Strategy: A marketplace rents you reach; a store builds you an asset. Etsy stays in the mix as a discovery channel, but The Quirky Gift Co is where I own the brand and the customer — so the audience I build compounds for me, not for the platform.

----

Design_explanation: Clean and modern, with plenty of white space so the products do the talking. The personality lives in the words and the designs, not in a busy layout — clear browse paths by category, by occasion and by best-seller keep the whole range easy to move through.

----

Build_explanation: Shopify and Liquid for the storefront, with made-to-order and print-on-demand fulfilment behind the personalised products. No custom back end to babysit — the point was a channel I could launch quickly and run single-handed.

----

Outcome: The Quirky Gift Co is a channel I own outright — my brand, my customer relationships and my margin, running next to Etsy rather than underneath it.

It's the same case I put to clients who sell on marketplaces or social platforms: rent the audience while it works, but build the thing you own. This one is me taking my own advice.
