Principles:

-
  title: Words before colours
  text: If a visitor can't tell what you do, the design has failed however nice it looks. We start with what you sell and who buys it, then design around that.
-
  title: One job per page
  text: Every page should help a visitor do one clear thing. Everything that doesn't help that gets cut, however much we like it.
-
  title: It has to work on a phone
  text: Most people will see your site on a phone, often on patchy signal. If it's awkward there, it isn't working, full stop.
-
  title: Honesty over upselling
  text: If you need less than you think, we'll tell you. We'd rather do a smaller job well than sell you one you don't need.

----

Working_style: You brief the person who builds the thing. We work in the open, share progress early, and would always rather ask an awkward question now than guess and get it wrong later. We're still around after launch.
