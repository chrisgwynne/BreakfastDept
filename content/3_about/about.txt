Principles:

-
  title: Words before colours
  text: If a visitor can't tell what you do, the design has failed however nice it looks. I start with what you sell and who buys it, then design around that.
-
  title: One job per page
  text: Every page should help a visitor do one clear thing. Everything that doesn't help that gets cut, however much I like it.
-
  title: It has to work on a phone
  text: Most people will see your site on a phone, often on patchy signal. If it's awkward there, it isn't working, full stop.
-
  title: Honesty over upselling
  text: If you need less than you think, I'll tell you. I'd rather do a smaller job well than sell you one you don't need.

----

Working_style: You brief the person who builds the thing — me. I work in the open, share progress early, and would always rather ask an awkward question now than guess and get it wrong later. And I'm still around after launch.
