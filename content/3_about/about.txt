How_heading: How I work

----

How_text: You brief me directly — the person who builds the thing. Work happens in the open, with progress shared early and awkward questions asked now rather than guessed at later. And I stay around after launch, watching what real visitors do and fixing what confuses them.

----

Principles:

-
  title: Words before decoration
  text: If a visitor can't tell what you do, the design has failed however nice it looks. It starts with what you sell and who buys it.
-
  title: One job per page
  text: Every page should help a visitor do one clear thing. Anything that doesn't help that gets cut.
-
  title: It has to work on a phone
  text: Most people arrive on a phone, often on patchy signal. If it's awkward there, it isn't working — full stop.
-
  title: Honesty over upselling
  text: If you need less than you think, you'll be told. A smaller job done well beats a bigger one you didn't need.
