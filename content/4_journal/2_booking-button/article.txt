Title: You don't need a booking system, you need a booking button

----

Excerpt: Small businesses talk themselves into expensive booking software when what they actually need is one obvious button in the right place. Here's the difference.

----

[{"id":"b01","type":"text","content":{"text":"<p>I get asked for booking systems a lot. Restaurants, barbers, gyms, consultants — all convinced they need a big piece of software to take bookings. Often they don't. They need a booking button that people can actually find. Be near the top. Follow the visitor down the page on a phone so it's always a thumb away. Go to somewhere that takes the booking without friction — even if that's just your existing calendar tool, or a phone number, or a simple form. The magic is in the placement, not the plumbing.</p>"}},{"id":"b06","type":"callout","content":{"variant":"info","title":"A useful order of operations","text":"<p>First make booking obvious with the tools you already have. Buy clever software later, if ever.","citation":""}},{"id":"b10","type":"text","content":{"text":"<p>Before you spend money on a booking system, ask a simpler question: can a customer on a phone book you in two taps right now?
