On the menu

What's on the menu.

Most people start with a call and we figure out which of these you actually need. Sometimes the answer is "less than you think".

  • A new website

    Built around what you sell, written in words a customer gets on the first read. The usual reason people call us.

  • A single page

    One offer, one thing to do. Handy when you're running ads or a launch and every click costs you money.

  • An online shop

    A Shopify store where finding a product and paying for it isn't a small ordeal. Fewer people give up at the checkout.

  • Fixing what you've got

    A site that gets visitors but no enquiries usually has a confusing bit. We find it and fix it — no full rebuild needed.

  • Getting found

    The unglamorous technical groundwork so people searching for what you do can actually land on your site.

  • A tidy-up

    Sharper words, type and colour for when the business has quietly outgrown the look it started with.

How it goes

Four steps. No drama.

You'll always know what's happening, what's next, and why it matters. We don't do mystery.

  1. 1

    We ask annoying questions

    What do you actually sell, who buys it, and why. The least glamorous part of the job and easily the most important.

  2. 2

    We decide what goes where

    We plan the page around the one thing a visitor needs to do. Everything that doesn't help that gets cut.

  3. 3

    We write it, then build it

    Words first, design around them. Fast, clear, and it works properly on a phone — because that's where most people will see it.

  4. 4

    We don't disappear

    We launch, watch what people actually do, and fix what's confusing. Launch is the start of the job, not the end of it.

What does a website cost?

The honest answer? It depends.

A local business that needs a simple website is a very different job from a retailer selling hundreds of products online. Some projects take a couple of weeks; others take several months. So rather than squeezing every business into a package, we scope each website around what it actually needs.

  1. 01

    Small business websites

    For businesses that need a professional site which clearly explains what they do and brings in enquiries.

    Usually something like

    Homepage · Services · About · Contact · proper on mobile · we write the words

  2. 02

    Ecommerce websites

    For businesses selling products online, where finding something and paying for it has to be effortless.

    Usually something like

    Shopify setup · product pages · collections · checkout improvements · customer journey planning

  3. 03

    Custom projects

    For businesses with more going on, where an off-the-shelf site won't cut it.

    Things like

    Booking systems · memberships · integrations · multi-location · bespoke functionality

Every project is quoted individually — no two businesses are the same. The best way to get a real sense of budget and timeline is to tell us a little about what you're trying to do.

The usual questions

Before you ask.

Everyone wants to know the same three things, really.

How long does it take?

A one-pager is usually about a week. A bigger site, three to five weeks depending on how much there is. We'll give you a date before we start and we keep to it.

How much, really?

It starts at the numbers above, and you get a fixed price before anyone does any work. No hourly meter ticking away, no invoice that's bigger than you expected.

Do you write the words too?

Yes — and we'd argue it's the most important part. We spend more time on the words than the colours, because that's what people actually read.

Will it show up on Google?

Every site gets the technical basics so it can be found. If you're in a crowded market and need to fight for it, that's more work — and a separate, honest conversation.

What happens after it's live?

We don't vanish. We watch what people do, fix what's confusing them, and you can keep us on a small monthly plan if you'd like a hand from time to time.

Can you fix the site I've got?

Usually, yes — most of our work is rebuilds and rescues. Send it through the form above and we'll tell you what's worth keeping and what isn't.

Thinking about a new website?

Tell us a little about your business and what you're hoping to achieve. Whether you're starting from scratch or replacing something that's holding you back, we'll let you know if we're the right people to help.
